PRIVACY POLICY
Checkout Speed Audit

What personal data the Checkout Speed Audit plugin's audit service and tvarkom's website handle, why, who processes it for us and how long we keep it.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The plugin's audit service
--------------------------

The Checkout Speed Audit plugin sends nothing until an administrator clicks "Send me the Report". Then:

* Registration (first audit only): your store's address, the report email and the address of the plugin's verification endpoint. We fetch a one-time token from that endpoint to confirm the request comes from your store, then issue a key for your domain. Unfinished registrations are deleted after 7 days.

* Each audit: your store's address, its cart and checkout page addresses and the report email. The request also reaches us from your store's server IP address, which we use for our limits.

* Store data, with each report request: PHP, WordPress and WooCommerce versions; database, options table and autoloaded options sizes; the number of active plugins and the object cache type; background task (Action Scheduler) counts and how late WP-Cron runs; the number of orders in the last 30 days as a range (for example 50–200); the names and sizes of the largest autoloaded options and of database tables left by removed plugins; PHP settings such as the memory limit; failed and late background task names, with error messages stripped of email addresses, links, IP addresses and long numbers; and a profile of one checkout page load on your store's server, with a temporary cart: plugin, theme and hook names with their timings, database query shapes with every value replaced by "?", the host names of outside services called, and object cache hit and miss counts.

* Business context, with each report request, so the report can explain its findings in business terms: your store's country and currency; the number of paid and on-hold orders in the last 30 days, and of failed, pending and cancelled orders; gross sales in the last 30 days and the average order value, as euro ranges only (for example €5,000–25,000: the plugin converts them on your store with the European Central Bank's daily rates and never sends the amounts); how many customers in the last 30 days were new and how many returning, as two counts; the number of published products and variations and whether stock management is on; the IDs of the enabled payment gateways and shipping methods (never their settings or keys); whether checkout uses the Checkout block or the classic shortcode and whether High-Performance Order Storage is on; the database and web server product; how many of your theme's WooCommerce template overrides are outdated; how many plugins have an update waiting; and the name of an active backup plugin, if any; the domain your store's emails come from (never the address); how many products were added to the cart, checkout pages opened and orders placed over the last 30 days, which the plugin counts on your store's server as daily totals, without cookies, browser scripts or anything about the shopper, keeping 35 days and deleting them when you uninstall it; and how many orders in the last 30 days came from each source type and from the 10 most common campaign sources, from WooCommerce Order Attribution.

The plugin lists the store data and the business context above the "Send me the Report" button. They never include customer names, email addresses or postal addresses, order contents, exact sales amounts, option contents or task arguments. For a sole trader, sales ranges can still relate to a person: that's why we receive ranges, not amounts, and use them across stores only as totals.

* Next scan date and exchange rates: when you open the plugin's page and before each audit, the plugin asks us when your store may scan again. Our answer includes the European Central Bank's daily euro rates, which we fetch from the ECB ourselves; your store doesn't contact the ECB.

What the scan reads. Our scanner then loads your store's public homepage, cart and checkout pages the way a visitor would, without running their JavaScript: it measures response times, page size and caching, and reads response headers and public platform markers. It doesn't log in, submit forms, place orders or read anything that isn't public.

What a report contains. Your store's address, the health score and risk level, the findings and recommendations, and the measurements: response times, page weight, files, caching and marketing trackers. The report also contains the store data and what we found in it. A report never contains your email address.

One report a week. Each store can request one free report per week. We use the store's audit requests to apply this.

Where your report goes. We email it to the report email, with a copy to our report mailbox (checkoutspeedaudit@tvarkom.com), so we can answer questions about it and keep a record of what we sent. We delete those copies 12 months after they're sent. Our database keeps no copy of the report or of the store data once it's sent.

Your store's profile. We keep the latest business context of each store (not the report, the store data or the checkout profile), replaced by each new report request, to understand which stores use the plugin: we only ever look at it as totals across stores, for example how many stores per country, size or checkout type, never store by store.

Legal basis (Art. 6 GDPR): sending, scanning and reporting is providing the service you ask for when you click "Send me the Report" (Art. 6(1)(b)); the store profile and its statistics, and the IP address and our limits, are our legitimate interests in understanding who uses our free plugin and preventing abuse of it (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time: email privacy@tvarkom.com and we'll delete your store's profile, or stop running scans or uninstall the plugin, and nothing more is sent.

Emails we send
--------------

We email reports to the report email, from checkoutspeedaudit@tvarkom.com, with a copy to that same mailbox. Once an email reaches your inbox, it stays there until you delete it.

If something goes wrong, for example our scanner can't reach your store or a report can't be delivered, our team gets an alert in our mailbox naming your store and the report email, so we can fix it or run the audit by hand. Alerts never contain your report.

tvarkom's website
-----------------

tvarkom.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no browser storage, and it has no forms. When you visit it, our hosting provider and Cloudflare process your IP address and the pages you request to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)).

When you email us
-----------------

We keep your messages and contact details to answer you and, if you become a client, to do the work. Legal basis: taking steps at your request or performing a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes data for us
-------------------------

* Fly.io: hosts our website, our API and our scanner, with their databases and logs, on servers in Amsterdam, Netherlands.

* Cloudflare: DNS and network protection for tvarkom.com.

* Resend: delivers our emails, including the attached reports. It keeps delivery information under its own retention policy, which we don't set.

* Google: Google Workspace hosts our mailbox.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU, they process it under an EU adequacy decision, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or Standard Contractual Clauses. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. We don't take payments.

How long we keep it
-------------------

These periods are applied automatically by our software:

* Failed audits (the request and any store data sent with it): 15 days after they failed. If our scanning service is restarted in the middle of an audit too many times, it keeps that audit for our engineers to inspect for 15 days.

* Audit requests (store, page addresses and time): 24 months, for our limits and records. The email address and IP address in them are erased after 90 days.

* Plugin registrations (store and the latest report email): the email is deleted after 12 months without activity, and the registration 24 months after the store's last audit. Activity means a successful audit, a registration or a reconnection.

* Store profiles: replaced by each report request, and deleted with the store's registration (at the latest 24 months after its last audit), or sooner on request.

* Exchange rates: only the latest day's.

Other copies:

* Our report mailbox (checkoutspeedaudit@tvarkom.com) holds a copy of each report email, with the store data in it, for 12 months after it's sent; then it's deleted.

* Our other mailboxes hold alerts, copies of past waitlist sign-ups and enquiries, and the emails you send us. They have no automatic deletion: we keep these messages for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any follow-up, and longer only where the law requires it, for example for records of work we do for you.

* Database snapshots: our hosting provider takes daily snapshots of our databases, plus one before each update, and deletes each after 5 days. Data we've deleted can remain in a snapshot until it expires. We use snapshots only to restore our service, and if we restore one, we delete again anything that had already been deleted.

* Logs: our service logs record store addresses and technical identifiers, never report contents or email addresses. Our hosting provider keeps them for about 7 days.

* Our scanning service keeps a working copy of a report only while the scan runs and the report is emailed.

* Google Analytics: tvarkom.com used Google Analytics, only with visitors' consent, until November 2026. Data collected then is kept for 14 months.

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email privacy@tvarkom.com and we'll reply within one month. For help with the plugin or a report, email support@tvarkom.com.

You can also complain to the Lithuanian supervisory authority, the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt (https://vdai.lrv.lt/en/)), or to the authority where you live.
