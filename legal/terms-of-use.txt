What the service does
---------------------

When you click Partial Scan or Full Scan, Tvarkom audits your store and emails you a report as a PDF. Our scanner loads your store's public pages like a visitor would and measures response times, page weight and caching. A Partial Scan uses only these measurements. A Full Scan also uses data the plugin reads from your store, including a profile of one checkout page load on your store's server, as described in our Privacy Policy (privacy-policy.txt, shipped with the plugin). Neither the scanner nor the plugin changes anything on your store. Results are sent only by email; the plugin doesn't show them on its page.

Reports describe your store at the moment of the audit. Results can vary between audits with traffic, hosting and caching.
